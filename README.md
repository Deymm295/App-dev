# My Favorites

## Favorite Series: Welcome to Derry
*Welcome to Derry* serves as a chilling prequel to the *IT* films. Set in the 1960s, it dives deep into the dark history of Derry, Maine, and explores the origins of the shape-shifting entity known as Pennywise the Dancing Clown before the events of the modern movies.
*   **Genre:** Horror / Supernatural Thriller
*   **Why it stands out:** It expands the Stephen King lore with a nostalgic yet terrifying atmosphere.

## Favorite Movie: The Day of the Jackal
*The Day of the Jackal* is a masterclass in suspense. It follows a brilliant, methodical assassin known only as "The Jackal" as he meticulously prepares to assassinate French President Charles de Gaulle, while a persistent investigator races against the clock to uncover his identity and stop him.
*   **Genre:** Political Thriller / Espionage
*   **Why it stands out:** The incredible attention to detail and realistic, slow-burn tension make it one of the most gripping thrillers ever made.
